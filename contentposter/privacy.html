Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Adventureland ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Content Poster application and the website at adventureland.company. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect a TikTok account to Content Poster, we collect:

  • Your TikTok username and display name
  • OAuth access tokens required to publish content on your behalf
  • Post metadata including timestamps, media counts, and publishing status

We do not collect passwords, private messages, follower lists, or analytics data from your TikTok account.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Publish photo slideshows and other content to your connected TikTok accounts
  • Display posting history and account status on your dashboard
  • Monitor for errors and improve service reliability

3. Data Storage and Security

OAuth tokens are stored securely and encrypted at rest. Post metadata is stored in JSON format and served only to authenticated users. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

Content Poster integrates with the TikTok API provided by ByteDance. When you connect your account, you authorize data exchange between Adventureland and TikTok in accordance with TikTok's Privacy Policy. We also use GitHub Pages for hosting, which is subject to GitHub's Privacy Statement.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account connection data and post history for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. Upon deletion, we remove all stored tokens and post metadata within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Disconnect your TikTok account at any time
  • Revoke access via TikTok's app settings

7. Children's Privacy

Content Poster is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, contact us at josh@adventureland.company.
